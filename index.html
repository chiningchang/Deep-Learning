Decades of ideas

How did we get here?

Today’s deep learning grew from decades of ideas.

ANN foundations · 1943 & 1958

McCulloch and Pitts described an early artificial neuron model in 1943. Rosenblatt’s 1958 perceptron paper was another milestone in learning from examples.

Selected milestones, not exact starting dates. These ideas developed along overlapping paths; newer designs did not simply replace older ones. All five belong to artificial neural networks, and LSTM is a type of RNN. Spacing shows order, not elapsed time.

How the designs differ
ArchitectureOrganizing ideaCommon tasks
Feedforward ANNTransform inputs through layersTabular regression and classification
CNNReuse local filters across locationsImage classification and object detection
RNNPass a state through ordered stepsTime series, speech, sequence modeling
LSTMManage state through learned gatesSequence tasks with longer dependencies
TransformerCombine token information using attentionLanguage, vision, and multimodal tasks

In plain language

Architecture
The way the parts of a neural network are arranged and connected.
Tabular data
Information organized into rows and columns, like a spreadsheet.
Time series
Observations recorded in order over time, such as daily temperatures.
Multimodal
Working with more than one kind of information, such as text and pictures.

These are common uses, not exclusive categories or a ranking. Choose a model based on the task, data, resources, and evaluation.
