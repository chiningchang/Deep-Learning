# Credits and Sources

## Teaching materials

The lessons are based on teaching materials provided by **Dr. Chi-Ning (Nick) Chang**, including the ANN course handout, *Beyond ANN: CNN, RNN, and LSTM*, and *Transformer and Generative AI*.

The figures below were supplied as part of those teaching materials. The website preserves the supplied images and adds explanatory notes and clickable diagram guides around them.
